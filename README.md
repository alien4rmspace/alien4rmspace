# Hi, I'm Damian 👋

CS student at the University of the Pacific, building robots that see, fly, and walk.
Outside of robotics, I develop games, with a focus on multiplayer systems and AI.

## 🛠️ What I'm building

**[Jetson Gesture Drone](https://github.com/alien4rmspace/jetson-gesture-drone)**: an autonomous drone you control with your body, hands, and voice
- Pose estimation + DeepStream tracking at 30 fps on a Jetson Orin Nano
- Person-following yaw control that keeps the camera centered on the tracked person
- Voice commands ("Hey Bart…") from an iPhone, parsed by a local Gemma LLM with schema-constrained output and confirmation for critical commands like landing
- Next up: MAVLink telemetry service, flight-log analysis, Livox Mid-360 lidar mapping

**Trash-Picking Hexapod** *(in progress)*: a six-legged robot that picks up litter
- Feetech STS3215 bus servos, tripod/ripple gait switching based on load
- RGB-D camera on the arm for grasping and curb detection, 2D lidar for SLAM
- Grasping policy trained with RL

**Just Scavenge**: co-op open-world zombie survival RTS (Unity/C#)
- Server-authoritative multiplayer (Netcode for GameObjects) on PlayFab dedicated servers
- ~36 player-controlled units per instance, NavMesh horde AI with sleep states, distance-based network culling
